    Expected rates come from each household's age mix, size, gender mix, ancestry (inferred from names) and unit/house, blended with each half's (north/south of O'Herns Rd) actual results so far in proportion to doors knocked (north −2.1 points with 868 doors, south +0.8 with 2,916), using knocks to 29 Oct 2026, one result per enrolled dwelling and the effect of individual volunteers removed. Maps crossing a suburb boundary sit in the suburb most of their enrolled dwellings are in. Age and ancestry composition rank maps by how much that one factor lifts or lowers expected support against the district average: age uses the median age of each household’s voters on a smooth scale, ancestry the main inferred ancestry. Maps with fewer than 10 enrolled addresses are greyed out. A map is finished when 95% or more of its enrolled street addresses have been knocked, or fewer than 5 are left (as on the progress map).
