Early voting centres: every centre where we expect at least 1.5% of Thomastown's early votes, whatever district the VEC lists it under (Thomastown voters go wherever is convenient). Election-day booths: the VEC's list for Thomastown, leaving out any where Thomastown voters cast under 10% of the votes. Colours show support among Thomastown voters at each centre compared with the average across Thomastown's centres, in narrower steps than the Northern Metro map so differences show: Weak (more than 5% below), Below average, Average (up to 5% above), Good (5–10% above), Strong (10–15% above), Very strong (15%+ above). How to use this page

This page shows which early voting centres (EVCs) and election-day booths matter most for VS in Thomastown.

How to use it

  1. Switch between "Early voting centres" and "Election day booths" with the buttons.
  2. "Support figures from": Doorknock model (default) uses our doorknock model only. Model + past VS votes blends it 50/50 with VS's past results in the voters' area (2022 state lower and upper house, 2025 Senate, and 2025 House where VS ran; geometric mean, strongest result counted twice). Best past VS result uses only the single strongest of those past results in each area, with no doorknock model.
  3. Each circle is a voting centre. The number is its priority for Thomastown (1 = most important). Click a circle, or a name in the list, to see its name, address and details.

What the circles mean

  • Size: how many votes we expect at the centre in total.
  • Colour: how likely Thomastown voters there are to support VS, from Weak (pale) to Very strong (dark), compared with the average across Thomastown's centres. Steps are 5% apart.
  • Dashed ring: another district also supplies a big share of its votes.
  • Square: the centre is outside Thomastown, but enough Thomastown voters go there that it matters.

How the priority is worked out

The ranking multiplies how many Thomastown voters will vote at each centre by how likely they are to support VS, counted twice (squared). Support is half the actual VS vote in the area at the 2022 state and 2025 federal elections, and half our doorknock model. Bigger, friendlier centres come first.

For shared centres, please talk to the neighbouring district's campaign so you can split the roster and make sure every shift is covered.
